# Image credits and licenses

Status of every image used by the Aviation packs. A question's `credit` field
(shown under the result after answering) is filled in only where the
photographer is known.

- **Confirmed**: photographer and license both established. All confirmed
  images are U.S. military photos. The file's embedded metadata names a U.S.
  Navy or U.S. Air Force photographer, so the image is a work of the U.S.
  federal government and is in the public domain.
- **Author only**: the embedded metadata names a photographer, but the license
  could not be confirmed. The credit is shown, but permission to use the image
  still needs checking.
- **Unknown**: no embedded metadata. Neither source nor license could be
  confirmed.

Most of these look like Wikimedia Commons images (the 960px and 500px widths
match Commons and Flickr thumbnail sizes). Commons could not be reached when
this list was made, so the remaining entries still need checking against their
Commons file pages.
